# Changelog

## 0.1.1 (2026-11-28)

### Rewards

- Points now have real value: 1 point for every R10 paid, and 100 points take R50 off a booking (5% back).
- Tiers follow what a client paid in the last 12 months: Member, Silver from R2,500 (1.2x points) and Gold from R5,000 (1.5x points). Using points never lowers a tier.
- Clients choose how many points to use when booking, up to half the price. Pay Now charges the reduced amount, and cancelled bookings return the points.
- Points expire 36 months after the last completed visit.
- The home page and Terms now describe the programme exactly as it works. The old perks that were never built (Platinum, 10% and 15% off) are gone.
- The owner dashboard shows unused points in rands, and the points discount on each booking.

### Sign-in

- "Continue with Google" on sign-in and sign-up, live once Google keys are added. "Continue with Apple" is built and switched off.
- Clients who sign up with Google or Apple accept the Terms on a short welcome step before booking.
- Fixed: new accounts were recorded as accepting the Terms automatically.
- Passwords need 8 characters instead of 15.

### Festive season

- A red Christmas hat on the Alora logo and a festive hero label, automatically from 1 December to 6 January.

### Testimonials

- Clients get a "visit complete" email with a link to leave a testimonial. The owner reviews them, adds photos and publishes them to the home page.

### Legal

- Privacy Policy and Terms & Booking Policy updated (effective 28 November 2026).
